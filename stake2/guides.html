Custody choices

How the major staking paths differ in practice.

The right method depends on technical comfort, liquidity needs, and trust assumptions.

Exchange-first path

Useful for speed and convenience, but combines counterparty exposure with opaque fee structures and policy risk.

Native wallet delegation

Offers clearer protocol alignment and better visibility into validator choice, though it requires more user attention.

Managed institutional service

Reduces operational workload while shifting focus toward provider governance, reporting, and contractual clarity.

What to track

Guide metrics that matter after the first deposit.

Monitoring is easier when you know which questions matter most.

Track realized yield instead of promotional yield, validator uptime instead of marketing claims, and withdrawal terms instead of vague promises about flexibility. Also note governance proposals, fee changes, restaking narratives, and any incident notices from the validator or service provider. These observations help you separate ordinary network changes from real provider quality issues, which is exactly the mindset strong educational staking material tries to cultivate.
