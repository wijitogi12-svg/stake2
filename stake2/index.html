About the site

Everstakes connects staking education, validator literacy, and ongoing ecosystem context.

The site is designed for readers who want more than a basic APY explanation. It explains what staking is, how rewards are produced, why provider quality matters, and how current industry developments can change the interpretation of a seemingly simple yield number.

Educational mission

Everstakes translates common lessons from leading staking explainers into a cleaner local learning path. Instead of presenting staking as effortless passive income, the site frames it as a balance between network participation, validator trust, liquidity constraints, and market risk.

Structured navigation

The homepage introduces the major concepts and then routes visitors toward dedicated pages for company information, contact details, learning modules, tools, policy pages, and editorial briefings. Every route is local so the experience stays coherent across the whole site.

Ecosystem perspective

Three angles Everstakes returns to across the site.

These sections synthesize the recurring themes found across widely read staking guides and service explainers.

Native staking

Useful for readers who want direct alignment with the protocol and more deliberate validator choice. Everstakes explains the extra responsibility that comes with that control.

Staking-as-a-service

Managed providers simplify infrastructure, which is why they appeal to institutions and advanced users. The site clarifies the added trust, reporting, and fee questions that come with the convenience.

Liquid staking

Flexible and increasingly important in DeFi conversations, yet harder to evaluate because smart contract exposure and peg behavior enter the risk model alongside core staking logic.
