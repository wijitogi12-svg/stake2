Privacy Notice

MethProtocol keeps its privacy stance simple, transparent, and education-first.

This privacy page explains the practical data assumptions behind a content-led staking site. MethProtocol does not position itself as a trading platform or wallet provider, so its privacy posture is intentionally narrower: collect only what is necessary to run the site, respond to readers, and deliver editorial updates. The page is written in direct language so visitors can understand it without legal training.

Collected details

What information the site may receive.

Most data comes from voluntary actions such as email submissions or direct messages.

MethProtocol may receive contact details when readers email the team, subscribe to an editorial digest, or request a correction. Basic technical signals such as device type, browser family, and aggregate traffic patterns may also be used to understand which guides are most useful and where navigation becomes confusing. The site does not need wallet access, seed phrases, or transaction authority to deliver its educational content, so visitors should never share that kind of sensitive information through contact channels.

How it is used

Why the collected information exists at all.

The purpose is operational clarity, not surveillance.

Newsletter delivery

Email addresses help send the requested digest or page update notice and are not collected to build unrelated marketing profiles.

Support and corrections

Messages allow the team to answer reader questions, review suggested edits, and improve guides when staking conditions or terminology change.

Site improvement

Aggregate page insights help identify which routes are used most often and which sections need clearer navigation or stronger explanations.

Reader control

How visitors can reduce or end data sharing.

A reader should be able to stop the flow easily.

Visitors can avoid optional submissions by browsing the site without using the newsletter form or direct contact channels. They can also request the removal of previously shared contact details by writing to the editorial inbox. MethProtocol aims to retain only what supports legitimate communication or record-keeping needs, and to remove unnecessary details once a request has been resolved. This keeps the privacy policy aligned with the narrow educational purpose of the site.
