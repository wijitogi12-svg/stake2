Editorial promise

Every guide is written to balance opportunity with infrastructure reality.

Everstakes content takes recurring lessons from mainstream staking explainers and reorganizes them into a tighter operational lens.

Across the site, we emphasize that staking rewards only make sense when readers also understand validator uptime, custody trade-offs, slashing exposure, unbonding periods, and the difference between nominal APY and net outcome after fees. That balance mirrors the best educational material in the wider staking ecosystem: staking can produce passive income and support network security, but results depend on asset selection, provider quality, market volatility, and governance changes. By presenting those ideas together, Everstakes helps readers compare exchange staking, native delegation, liquid staking, and managed validator services without assuming one model fits every risk profile.
