Response workflow

How Everstakes handles requests after they arrive.

The site is positioned as an information and news resource, so requests are triaged by intent rather than arrival order alone.

Editorial corrections are routed first because accuracy affects the entire learning path. Partnership requests are then matched with the director or marketing lead depending on whether the subject is research, events, or campaign planning. Questions about validator operations, staking-as-a-service workflows, or page functionality are shared with the development desk, which checks whether the answer belongs in a direct reply or should become a permanent improvement to a public guide. This workflow keeps the contact page useful for casual readers while also making it credible for operators, journalists, and B2B visitors.

Office hours

When to expect live feedback and what to include.

A strong message helps the team answer with fewer follow-ups.

  • Include the page name or local path when reporting outdated content.
  • Explain whether your question is about rewards, risks, validator quality, custody, or governance.
  • Add the network name and provider model if you are comparing staking options.
  • For press requests, share deadlines, publication details, and the angle you need the team to address.
