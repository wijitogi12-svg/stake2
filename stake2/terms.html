Terms of Use

Everstakes is an educational site, not a brokerage, custodian, or investment advisor.

The terms page explains how readers should interpret the site content and what behavior is expected when using the public routes, newsletter sign-up, and contact channels. Because the platform focuses on staking education and ecosystem context, the most important boundary is simple: information published here is designed to help readers understand the landscape, not to substitute for individualized legal, tax, or financial advice.

Content interpretation

How to read the material on this site.

The guides and articles are for learning and comparison only.

Everstakes content summarizes staking concepts, validator practices, and ecosystem developments so readers can perform their own due diligence more effectively. Nothing on the site should be treated as a guarantee of returns, a promise of safety, or a recommendation that a specific asset, protocol, or provider fits your situation. Visitors remain responsible for reviewing official network documentation, local rules, and professional advice where appropriate before acting on any staking idea.

Acceptable use

What the site expects from users of its public channels.

Respectful use protects the quality of the educational environment.

No abuse

Do not misuse the contact channels for spam, harassment, impersonation, or attempts to extract sensitive information from the team or other readers.

No false authority claims

Do not present Everstakes material as personalized legal, tax, or investment advice issued for a specific user account or portfolio.

No secret sharing

Never send private keys, seed phrases, or other sensitive credentials through the site or associated contact methods.

Updates and changes

Why content and routes may evolve over time.

Staking changes, so the educational layer must also change.

The site may revise pages when network rules, validator practices, or editorial priorities change. Route labels, learning module summaries, and FAQ language can all be updated to reflect better information or clearer structure. Everstakes aims to preserve continuity across the library so new material still connects to the home page, contacts page, and supporting guides. Continued use of the site after updates indicates acceptance of the current version of these terms.
